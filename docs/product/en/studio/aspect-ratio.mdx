# Aspect-Ratio Adaptation

The Aspect Ratio editor adapts a selected image for another placement. It automatically distinguishes a crop from an AI extension based on where the target frame sits relative to the source.

## Choose a target frame

Select a preset such as `1:1`, `4:5`, `3:4`, `4:3`, `16:9`, `9:16`, `3:2`, or `2:3`, or use a freeform frame. Move and resize the frame while zooming or panning the source. Edge snapping helps align the frame with the original image.

- A frame entirely inside the source creates a crop.
- A frame extending outside the source uses AI to invent the missing area.

The target dimensions are constrained to a practical maximum and shown before processing.

## Direct the extension

For AI extension, state what belongs in the new space and what must not change. Choose a compatible Gemini or OpenAI provider, model, format, quality, background, and image size when those controls apply.

## Verify the adapted asset

Inspect boundaries between original and generated pixels, repeated textures, stretched objects, unexpected people or text, and composition at the final placement size. Save the result as a new asset; do not overwrite the only copy of the source.
