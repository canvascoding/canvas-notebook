# Working with Email Focus

When your server administrator enables email preparation, Focus becomes the default view unless you have chosen Classic. Canvas prepares category, priority, spam likelihood and reply need in the background. You can choose Classic at any time; this changes your view while the server's preparation setting continues to apply.

Preparation covers every unread Inbox email regardless of age, plus all other Inbox emails within the configured lookback of at most 30 days, independently for each mailbox. Larger backlogs are prepared over multiple passes. Old read emails without an assessment remain available in All emails and Other emails; they are explicitly not selected for preparation and do not count as awaiting preparation. Existing assessments remain available after reading.

## Start from Home

With preparation enabled, the email card on the Workspace page shows the two highest-priority messages across all authorized personal and work mailboxes. Category, high or urgent priority, reply need and source help you choose what to open. Counts cover the full captured scope rather than just the two preview rows. Needs review and Not yet prepared open their respective groups; incomplete coverage remains visible.

Opening a preview loads the exact message from its own mailbox. Entering Focus this way does not change a saved Classic preference. Percentages and assessment details remain in the reader. Apply new assessments when ready; access that cannot be confirmed clears the preview. When preparation is disabled centrally, the classic unread-personal-mail preview remains available.

## Choose the inboxes you want to work on

Use Mailbox scope to select All mailboxes, Personal mailboxes, Work mailboxes or one specific mailbox. Work includes shared mailboxes assigned to workspaces you can access. The combined list shows each message's source; opening, replying and organizing use that source's permissions and sender.

Focus initially shows Important now and Reply needed. Needs review and Not yet prepared remain directly accessible with counts. Use All emails for the rest of the captured inbox messages, or expand More views and categories for category filters, spam and your completed items.

A blank Focus list can coexist with messages needing review or preparation. Check those groups and any coverage warning before treating the inbox as finished. New assessments offer Apply updates, so the list can stay in place while you work.

## Inspect a rating when you need it

Open a message to see its category, priority and a short reason. Assessment details reveals spam and reply likelihood and available choice probabilities. A dash means the value is unknown. Percentages are model estimates; a high number does not establish measured accuracy for your inbox.

Uncertain priority, spam or reply decisions and important/spam conflicts remain in Needs review. Missing, failed or outdated preparation remains visible for follow-up. Suspected spam is not automatically separated without a separately verified sorting profile. Authorized writers can correct a message's assessment; that correction applies to other readers of the same mailbox. Read-only readers can inspect it.

## Mark your own work as done

Mark as done for me removes an item from your active Focus work and makes it available under Done for me. Undo done returns it. Read-only work-mailbox readers can also use this personal status.

Opening a message can change its read state where permitted, but reading does not complete your work. Personal completion does not archive the message, mark it answered or close a team case. A reply-need estimate also does not prove whether somebody has already replied.

## Search and use Classic

Search indexed inboxes checks sender, subject and preview within your selected scope. The combined list covers the inbox messages Canvas has captured; preparation and coverage can be incomplete. For full provider search, other folders and older mail, select one mailbox and choose Classic. Classic in a combined scope still uses the captured inbox list.

Your Focus/Classic choice is saved. The Appearance menu's Distraction free option changes the layout separately from email prioritization.

## Keep the sender with your draft

New email in a combined scope asks you to choose an available sender. Replies keep the opened message's sender mailbox. Once opened, a draft keeps that identity and its attachment context while you change scope or view. Minimize it to keep working, then choose Resume draft. If sender access is lost, the draft remains available to inspect, while sending and AI actions require valid access.

## If preparation pauses

Saved assessments remain available during a model-provider problem or an exhausted preparation budget. New messages may remain unprepared. If the email provider itself is unavailable, a captured row may still appear while its full message cannot load. Follow the warning, retry or open that mailbox in Classic. Access that cannot be confirmed clears the previous view.
