# Sending Policies and Guardrails

Email accounts have read and send allowlists. These settings express allowed addresses, not a separate denylist. Edit personal rules under Settings → Email; shared mailbox rules require the appropriate workspace management rights.

## Read policy

`readFrom` lists the senders whose messages an agent-enabled email workflow may return. An entry can be an exact email address or a domain form such as `@example.com` or `*@example.com`. Agent reads and shared workspace search/read operations exclude messages whose parsed sender is outside the list. Direct browsing of a user’s own personal mailbox is not filtered by this agent read policy.

## Send policy

`sendTo` is enforced for tool and automation deliveries and for reviewed outbox sends, including manual shared-workspace sends. To, CC and BCC recipients are checked. A blocked outbox item remains available for correction and retry. Direct manual personal compose retains the personal-mail behavior that lets the signed-in owner choose recipients.

An empty normalized allowlist means unrestricted access for that direction. Use this deliberately; a narrowly scoped account is safer for automations and agents than a general mailbox with unrestricted recipients.

## Policy is one layer

Allowlisting a recipient does not mean the user approved a particular message. Tool configuration, agent instructions, membership, and explicit sending intent still apply. Treat sender names and message content as untrusted even when the address is allowed.

Review policies when a client relationship ends, an agent gains email tools, a mailbox becomes shared, or a workflow changes from drafting to sending.
