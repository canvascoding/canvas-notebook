# Navigating Canvas Notebook

Canvas Notebook groups its main work areas in the app switcher. The apps and settings shown to you can vary with your account and permissions.

If this is your first time here, see [First-run setup for administrators and users](/docs/first-run-setup) to understand the initial server setup, personal onboarding, and the permissions that affect what you can see.

## Open an app

1. Choose **Open apps** in the top bar.
2. Use **Quick access** for **Notebook**, **Automations**, **To-dos**, **Email**, or **Studio**.
3. Choose **More apps** to find **Files**, **Canvas Chat**, **Document Graph**, **Security**, **Settings**, **Help**, or **Browser Lab**.

The home page also offers shortcuts such as **Open notebook**, **Open inbox**, **Open Studio**, and **Open automations** for the active workspace.

## Work in a Notebook workspace

The Notebook workspace brings chat and files together. The selected workspace appears in the top bar. In the Notebook view, use **Chat** to start or continue an agent conversation and **Files** to browse workspace documents. To create a Markdown document, open **Files**, choose **Create → New file**, enter a filename, choose a destination, and choose **Create file**.

An agent’s ability to read or change a file depends on its workspace access and the current account’s permissions. See [Workspace and project access](/docs/agents/workspace-project-access) for access details.

## Find settings

Open **Open apps → More apps → Settings**. Settings are grouped by area:

- **Account**: general options, mobile app, and memory.
- **Workspace**: workspace and brand design.
- **Agents & Tools**: agent settings, browser, and plugins.
- **Connections**: integrations, MCP, and email.
- **System & Administration**: secrets, AI providers and models, users, usage, license, updates, and other administrator options.

Some settings are administrator-only. If a section or control is missing, ask an administrator to check your role and workspace permissions.

## Choose a workspace

The workspace selector in the top bar identifies the current workspace. Workspace context matters: files, agent access, email accounts, and automation jobs can be scoped differently. Check the selected workspace before creating or changing content. Learn more in [Workspaces overview](/docs/collaboration/workspaces-overview).
